# Stages and gates

## Stages

| Stage | Means |
|---|---|
| `activate` | Someone finds Pedal and takes a first ride |
| `motivate` | They see enough value to pay |
| `sustain` | Riding becomes a habit |
| `change` | It changes how they get around |

## Gates

| Gate | Name | Stage | What the person decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Open the app | activate | Whether it's worth trying |
| G2 | Unlock a first bike | activate | Whether to ride now |
| G3 | Choose a plan | motivate | Pay as you go, monthly or annual |
| G4 | Ride again within a week | sustain | Whether to come back |
| G5 | Commute by bike | change | Whether to switch their daily trip |
