# Riders decide whether to pay at their first unlock, mostly on price

## What we saw

9 of 12 riders said they compared plans before their first ride. P04: "I wanted to know what it'd cost me before I got on."

## What it doesn't tell us

All 12 were recruited from the beta, so they had already chosen to try it.
