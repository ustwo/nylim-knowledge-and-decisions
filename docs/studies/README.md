# Studies

A study is a piece of research we run on purpose, such as a round of interviews, a concept test or a survey. It's one file here, named by date: `2026-11-04-adviser-interviews.md`. Start from [`_template.md`](_template.md).

Studies are optional. Findings can come from any [source](../sources.md), and most will come from documents we're given. A study earns a file when it's worth planning: when it's meant to settle something.

## The rules

1. **No study without a question.** Before it starts, a study names the open decisions and hypotheses it's for. The [Test next](../_generated/evidence.md#test-next) list is the best place to look: important hypotheses with little evidence either way.
2. **Say what would change.** For each question, say what we'd do differently depending on the answer.
3. **Its raw material is a source.** Register the notes, recordings or results in [`sources.md`](../sources.md), and draw findings from them in the usual way.
4. **It ends by saying what it means** for each question: closed, narrowed, or a new question opened. The evidence view will show the rest.
