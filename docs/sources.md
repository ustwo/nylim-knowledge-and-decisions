# Sources

What the team was given and has gathered, and what each source governs. Each source has an ID, such as SRC-04, so findings can cite it. A source is an input: it becomes a commitment only through a decision, and evidence only through a finding.

Sources stay where they live, in Drive, Miro, Notion, a research tool or an analytics platform. This page registers them, and only the findings drawn from them come into the repo. See [findings](findings/README.md#getting-findings-from-a-source).

## What governs

- **{{The contract or statement of work}} governs scope, deliverables and timeline.** Where any other source conflicts with it, it wins.
- **{{The baseline}} holds wherever no decision says otherwise** (PRD-03). A departure from it needs a decision that says what changed and why.
- **Everything else is an input.** Documents written before the work started, and asks made in conversation, are expectations to weigh, not commitments.

## The sources

Take the next number for SRC, with two digits, and never reuse one. Newest first within each group. Mark a source **internal** when it holds commercial or personal context. Internal sources are never quoted in the PRD, a decision or a finding, though a finding may cite one by ID.

Research material, such as recordings, transcripts and interview notes, is registered here as internal and stays in the research tool. Analytics exports are registered the same way, one row per export or dashboard.

When a newer source replaces an older one, keep both rows, and say in the older one's Governs column what replaced it.

When a copy is kept in the repo, such as a roadmap captured from a sheet, its row says where the original lives, and the original stays the source of truth.
